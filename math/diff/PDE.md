#### method of characteristics
find lines along which the equation becomes an ODE and then solve that ODE
## laplace transform
given a PDE in two independent variables $x,t$, we use the laplace transform on one of the variable to obtain an ODE which we solve and then use the inverse laplace transform to find the solution to the original problem

## common equations
### heat equation
$$\dot{u}=\alpha^{2}\nabla^{2}u$$
### wave equation
$$\ddot{u}=c^{2}\nabla^{2}u$$
### laplace equation
$$\nabla ^{2}u=0$$
### poisson equation
$$\nabla^{2}\varphi=f$$
### diffusion equation
$$\dot{\phi}=\nabla \cdot \begin{bmatrix}
D(\phi,\vec{r})&\nabla \phi
\end{bmatrix}$$
### helmoltz equation
$$\nabla^{2}f=-k^{2}f$$
