# #english-note 
## graphs
a graph $G$ consists of 2 sets:
- $V$ whose elements are vertices of $G$ 
- $E$ whose elements are unordered pairs from $V$, those element are edges of $G$
### multigraph
for some purposes, $E$ can be a multiset instead of a set
an element that appears more than once in $E$ is a multiedge
a graph that has at least one multiedge is a multigraph
### loop
an edge of the form $(v,v)$ for some $v\in V$ is called a loop
### simple graph
a simple graph is a graph that has no loop or multiedge
