## scientific method
steps:
1. make an observation
2. ask a question
3. form a hypothesis, or testable explanation
4. make a prediction based on that hypothesis
5. test the prediction
6. iterate; use the result to make new hypotheses or predictions

## science fiction
### elements of fiction
#### characters
main character = protagonist
adversary = antagonist

round characters: rich backstor and complex personalities
flat characters: one main personality trait, stock characters

#### pov
first person: protagonist mind
second person: reader as the main character
third person limited: perspective of only one character
third person omniscient: perspective of multiple characters at the same time
#### setting
time period
location
#### plot
expositon: introduction
rising action: event that generate conflict
climax: turning point
falling action: events after the climax's tension is released
denouement: ending
#### theme
reccuring idea or main message
tend to be universal
usually implicit

#### soft sci-fi

#### military sci-fi
